How to use this

About 30 minutes, free, and no coding.

  1. Replace the example details with yours.

    Open each section on the left and type over the example couple's details. The preview updates as you type. Use the On site switch to hide any section you don't need.

  2. Add your photos.

    A background photo under Couple & date, and a few portrait photos under Photo gallery.

  3. Optional: make an RSVP form.

    Create a free form at forms.google.com, click Send → link icon → Copy, and paste it under RSVP → Form link.

  4. Download your site.

    Click Download site. You get one file, index.html, which is your whole website. Don't rename it.

  5. Put it online with Netlify (free).
    • Put index.html inside a new folder on your computer.
    • Go to app.netlify.com/drop and sign up first (free). Sites dropped without an account are deleted within an hour.
    • Drag the folder onto the page. You get a link ending in .netlify.app to share with guests.

Your details are kept in this browser only. Use Save settings file at the bottom of the form to keep a backup with your photos.
